Passion Project: Exploring Brain Activation Patterns in fMRI Data

This project uses open fMRI datasets (Haxby via Nilearn) to visualize brain responses to stimuli and connects patterns to neurological disorders like Alzheimer's.

Key Visualizations

Methods

  • Data: Haxby dataset and OpenNeuro
  • Tools: Python with Nilearn in Google Colab
  • Visualized activation in regions like fusiform face area and hippocampus
  • Literature review on Alzheimer's biomarkers

Results & Insights

Normal brains show strong localized activation. In Alzheimer's, reduced hippocampal activity disrupts memory-related patterns, highlighting fMRI's role in early diagnosis.
